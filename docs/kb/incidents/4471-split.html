How to tell them apart

Read the dates in the report, then look up the tenant (src/lib/data/tenants/). Every tenant on file writes the day first.

  • Day and month swapped, any channel except a booking-v3 form: parser. 7 April comes out as 4 July. Reports through booking-v2 forms land here too: v2’s hint says DD/MM/YYYY, so people type day first and the parser flips it.
  • Day and month swapped, through a booking-v3 form: hint. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the field, people type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Only alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour run booking-v3.
  • The date moved by one day: still parser. 12 May shown as 11 May, Tuesday shown as Monday. The desk lists these in desk-notes.json, files them under parser by tenant and footnotes them; they have no ticket of their own.

Do not go by the intake tag. Intake guesses it from the reporter’s browser before anyone reads the report, and it names the wrong problem on 38 of these.

Why one ticket held two

Decision 0001 merges on subject line. Both problems arrive as “Wrong day on the invite” or “Month and day swapped?”, so subject matching put them in one thread, and the queue then read as one bug. The subject says how the reporter felt; the dates in the body and the tenant say which bug it is.

Where it stands

The desk closed #4471 on 5 October, and each ticket here is closed the same way:

  • parser: fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales ships the parser fix. #1 is closed as completed.
  • hint: won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired, so its hint is gone with it. #2 is closed as not planned.

0001 still merges on subject. A new decision record has to replace it before the next incident.
