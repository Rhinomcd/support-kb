#4471 is three problems with three different fixes. The parser fix alone leaves 38 of these reports broken.

How to tell them apart

Read the dates in the report, then look up the tenant (src/lib/data/tenants/). Every tenant on file writes the day first.

  • Day and month swapped, any channel except a booking-v3 form: parser. 7 April comes out as 4 July. Reports through booking-v2 forms land here too: v2’s hint says DD/MM/YYYY, so people type day first and the parser flips it.
  • Day and month swapped, through a booking-v3 form: hint. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the field, people type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Only alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour run booking-v3.
  • The date moved by one day: shift. 12 May shown as 11 May, Tuesday shown as Monday, an invite that arrived before the booking. No swap produces that. The desk already listed these in desk-notes.json; the #4471 table files them under parser and footnotes them.

Do not go by the intake tag. Intake guesses it from the reporter’s browser before anyone reads the report, and it names the wrong problem on 38 of these.

Why one ticket held three

Decision 0001 merges on subject line. All three problems arrive as “Wrong day on the invite” or “Month and day swapped?”, so subject matching put them in one thread, and the queue then read as one bug. The subject says how the reporter felt; the dates in the body and the tenant say which bug it is.

What changes

  • Fix the parser and the booking-v3 hint separately. Ship the hint fix with the parser fix, or booking-v3 tenants stay wrong.
  • Investigate the one-day shift as its own bug: it looks like a time-zone conversion, not parsing.
  • When a date report comes in, match it to a ticket by its problem, not its subject. 0001 needs a new decision record to replace it.
